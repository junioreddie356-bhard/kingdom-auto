OUR STORY

Kingdom Automobile Dealership was founded with a simple mission: to help people in Northern Ghana get more car for their money.

My name is Akudugu Asiedu, and I am the founder and lead specialist. I hold a degree in Natural Resources from the University for Development Studies (UDS), Class of 2025. While my academic background is in natural resources, my professional journey has taken me deep into the world of international trade.

For the past three years, I have worked extensively in the international market, specializing in import and export with a deep focus on logistics and shipment. I have hands-on experience navigating the complexities of global supply chains—from sourcing products and negotiating with suppliers to coordinating shipping and managing customs clearance.

This experience gave me a unique perspective. I saw how businesses and individuals in Northern Ghana were often overpaying for goods simply because they lacked access to international markets. Cars, in particular, stood out. Local dealerships offered limited options at high prices, and the import process seemed intimidating to most people.

I decided to bridge that gap.

In 2025, I founded Kingdom Automobile Dealership—a vehicle import agency based right here in Tamale. My goal is to help individuals, families, and businesses in Northern Ghana import quality used cars from China, Korea, Japan, Dubai, and the USA at 20–40% below local dealer prices.

WHY I STARTED THIS BUSINESS

I started Kingdom Automobile Dealership because I saw a gap in the market. People in Tamale were paying too much for cars. They were either overpaying at local dealerships or struggling to navigate the complex import process on their own.

I have the knowledge, experience, and connections to change that. I understand international markets. I understand logistics and shipping. I understand customs clearance and Ghana's import regulations. I combined all of that to create a service that makes car import simple, transparent, and affordable for the people of Northern Ghana.

MY PROMISE TO YOU

When you work with me, you're not just getting a car—you're getting peace of mind. I handle everything from start to finish. I give you full transparency on costs. I ensure your car meets all Ghana regulations. I deliver it to your doorstep in Tamale.

My business is built on trust, integrity, and professional service. I don't believe in hidden fees or shortcuts. I believe in honesty, hard work, and building long-term relationships with my clients.
