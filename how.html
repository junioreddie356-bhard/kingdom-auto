HOW IT WORKS

Your Step-by-Step Guide to Importing a Car with Kingdom Automobile Dealership

Importing a car might seem complicated. But with us, it's simple. We handle everything from start to finish, so you don't have to stress about shipping, paperwork, or clearing at the port.

Here is how the process works in 10 simple steps.

STEP 1: CONTACT US

Reach out to us via WhatsApp, phone, or through our website. Tell us:

  • Your budget
  • Preferred make and model (e.g., Toyota Corolla, Honda Accord, SUV)
  • Year range (e.g., 2020–2023)
  • Mileage preference
  • Any specific features you want (sunroof, leather seats, 4WD, etc.)

We listen carefully to understand your needs. The more details you give us, the better we can find the perfect car for you.

STEP 2: WE SOURCE THE CAR

Once we know what you're looking for, we search for the best options from our network of trusted suppliers in:

  • 🇨🇳 China
  • 🇰🇷 Korea
  • 🇯🇵 Japan
  • 🇦🇪 Dubai
  • 🇺🇸 USA

We use verified platforms and work with reputable exporters. We check the VIN, service history, and vehicle condition before we recommend any car to you.

We send you photos, videos, and a full spec sheet so you can see exactly what you're getting.

STEP 3: WE GIVE YOU A FULL COST BREAKDOWN

Before you commit, we provide you with a transparent cost breakdown that includes:

Cost ComponentWhat It Covers
FOB PriceThe car price (including export license)
Shipping & InsuranceOcean freight to Tema Port + marine insurance
CIF ValueThe total used by GRA to calculate duties
Import DutyBased on engine size (5–20%)
VAT, NHIL, GETFund, LeviesGovernment taxes and fees
Clearing Agent & Port ChargesPaperwork, handling, and examination at Tema Port
Transport to TamaleInland haulage from Tema to Tamale
Our Service FeeOur transparent charge for handling everything

No hidden fees. No surprises. You see every cedi before you say yes.

STEP 4: YOU PAY THE DEPOSIT

If you're happy with the cost breakdown, you pay a deposit to secure the car and begin the sourcing process.

We offer flexible payment plans (3, 6, or 12 months) to make the process more affordable.

The deposit is non-refundable and covers the initial sourcing and shipping costs.

STEP 5: WE HANDLE ALL PAPERWORK

We coordinate with the supplier to ensure all documents are ready:

  • Bill of Lading
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Export License
  • Pre-Export Inspection Certificate (PVoC)
  • Certificate of Conformance (CoC)

We ensure every document is accurate and complete before the car leaves the country of origin.

STEP 6: WE ARRANGE SHIPPING

We book shipping from the origin country to Tema Port.

Shipping takes approximately 30–45 days depending on the origin.

We track the vessel and keep you updated on the car's progress.

STEP 7: WE CLEAR THE CAR AT TEMA PORT

When the car arrives at Tema Port, we work with our trusted clearing agents to:

  • Submit all documents to Customs
  • Pay import duties and taxes
  • Complete inspection and examination
  • Secure release of the vehicle

We handle everything so you don't have to stress about port processes or paperwork.

STEP 8: WE TRANSPORT THE CAR TO TAMALE

Once the car is cleared, we arrange for it to be transported from Tema to Tamale.

The car is loaded onto a truck and delivered safely to you.

Transport takes approximately 2–3 days.

STEP 9: YOU PAY THE BALANCE

Before delivery, you pay the remaining balance of the total landed cost.

If you're on a payment plan, this is when the final payment is due.

We accept bank transfers, mobile money, and cash.

STEP 10: YOU RECEIVE YOUR CAR

We deliver the car to your doorstep in Tamale. You inspect the car, confirm it meets your expectations, and we hand over the keys and all original documents.

Congratulations! You're now driving your dream car.
